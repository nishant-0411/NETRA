CD No. 1  |  Date: 23/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector Vikram Singh
Received formal complaint from the branch manager regarding unauthorized access to the core banking system. The victim, Samaksh Raghavan, reported a suspicious deduction of funds at 22:28 hours on 22/10/2024. Preliminary scan of the server logs indicates an internal terminal was used to authorize an RTGS transfer. I visited the branch at Nariman Point. The CCTV footage for the server room is partially corrupted, but I have secured the login logs for the terminal identified as "STATION-04". The timestamp of the breach aligns with the unauthorized transaction. I have requested the Nodal Officer of the bank to provide the employee shift schedule for the night of the incident.

CD No. 2  |  Date: 24/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector Vikram Singh
The bank's internal IT audit report received today suggests the credentials used belong to a junior administrator, Samarth N. (Samarth Narain). Upon questioning, Narain appeared visibly agitated and claimed his workstation was left unlocked during a brief tea break. However, digital forensic footprints show a remote desktop connection established from an external IP address just minutes before the transaction. I have initiated a CDR request for the mobile number ending in …45e5, registered to Narain. The witness, Manthan Chhabra, who was working the late shift, claims he saw a blue hatchback, possibly a Hyundai, parked near the service gate, bearing a plate partially reading …1316.

CD No. 3  |  Date: 26/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector Vikram Singh
CDR analysis for the phone ending in …45e5 (Narain) shows multiple calls to a suspicious number ending in …6223. This secondary number is linked to a user known as "The Ghost" in Narain’s call logs, though background checks reveal this individual is likely Warjas K. (Warjas Kulkarni). Further investigation into the financial trail confirms the siphoned funds reached a mule account at PNB, branch Ballia, held by a Vidhi Das. The transfer amount was 4,69,000 INR. I have requested a freeze on the PNB account ending in …6872.

CD No. 4  |  Date: 28/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector Vikram Singh
I conducted a surveillance sweep near the address associated with Warjas Kulkarni in Arrah. A blue Hyundai hatchback with registration UP-11-QK-1316 was spotted. The owner, Kulkarni, was not present, but a neighbor confirmed he frequently travels to Mumbai for "consultancy work." I am cross-referencing his travel history with the incident date. The location data (LAC/CI) for phone ending in …6223 places him in the vicinity of the Bandra-Kurla complex on the night of the cyber-heist, which contradicts his earlier statement to the local police that he was in Patna.

CD No. 5  |  Date: 30/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector Vikram Singh
Interrogation of Samarth Narain continued today. He finally admitted that "The Ghost" (Kulkarni) had provided him with a hardware token to bypass the two-factor authentication on the bank’s server. Narain claims he was pressured into the act due to mounting gambling debts. He also confessed that the funds were immediately converted into crypto-assets through an exchange based in a non-cooperative jurisdiction. I am currently working with the Cyber Cell to track the wallet addresses mentioned in Narain’s statement.

CD No. 6  |  Date: 01/11/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector Vikram Singh
Today, we successfully detained Warjas Kulkarni in Arrah. During a search of his vehicle, a silver HONDA motorcycle key was found (GJ 08 PB 1344), which is registered to the accomplice, Narain. This confirms the direct link between the bank insider and the primary hacker. We recovered a laptop from Kulkarni’s residence containing traces of the VPN software used to mask the remote access during the heist. The suspect remains evasive regarding the specific cryptocurrency exchange platform used for the laundering, but his device is now with the forensic unit for deep-packet inspection. Case proceeds to custodial remand.
