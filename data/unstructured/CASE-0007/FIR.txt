1. Acts and Sections: Sections 17 and 18 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), Section 25 of the Arms Act, and Section 120-B of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).

2. Place of Occurrence: Abandoned Industrial Unit, Sector-4 Industrial Area, Delhi. Distance from Police Station: Approx. 12 KM.

3. Complainant/Informant: SI Vikram Rathore, Special Cell, Delhi Police.

4. Details of the Incident:
On 01/10/2024 at approximately 15:20 hours, acting on credible intelligence received through technical surveillance regarding the movement of illicit arms and suspected terror financing, a specialized team conducted a raid at an abandoned factory premises located in the Sector-4 Industrial Area. Upon arrival, the team observed suspicious activity involving individuals who appeared to be transporting concealed cargo from a vehicle matching the description of a light-colored sedan with a partial license plate registration ending in …5421. 

Initial investigations revealed that the site was being utilized as a covert storage facility for arms procurement. Intelligence intercepts indicate that a sleeper cell, operating under various codenames—specifically the alias "Sahib" utilized by multiple associates including one Jagat Mallick (also known as Sahib) and a female associate known as Kashish Mitra—has been coordinating the logistics of this operation. Further scrutiny of bank trails identified an RTGS transaction of INR 1,70,000/- originating from an Axis Bank account held by one Oeshi Gulati (also using the alias Sahib) to an account held by K. Mitra, marked under the pretext of "Consulting Fees."

During the operation, the team identified another associate referred to as "Tiger" (later identified as Meghana Krishnan), who was observed utilizing a mobile number ending in …3654 to communicate with the primary suspects. The subject Mohammed Singh, whose details were linked to a vehicle with a plate ending in …8890, was also present in the vicinity of the industrial unit prior to the raid. 

The suspects managed to evade immediate capture by fleeing the premises in a vehicle bearing a partial registration plate …5421. A preliminary sweep of the abandoned factory floor uncovered evidence of clandestine logistical planning. The financial trail links these activities to hawala channels originating from overseas, intended for the procurement of prohibited weaponry.

5. Suspects: 
The individuals identified are Jagat Mallick (Alias: Sahib), Meghana Krishnan (Alias: Tiger), Oeshi Gulati (Alias: Sahib), and Kashish Mitra (Alias: Sahib). Their current whereabouts are unknown. All suspects are believed to be in possession of mobile devices, specifically numbers ending in …492e, …b0dd, …14f9, and …4682. 

6. Action Taken:
The information has been recorded, and the investigation is assigned to Inspector Arjun Singh. Digital forensic teams have been requested to analyze the tower dump data collected from the locality at the time of the incident. Further efforts are underway to track the banking beneficiary accounts held at the Axis Bank branches in Sonipat and Pali.
