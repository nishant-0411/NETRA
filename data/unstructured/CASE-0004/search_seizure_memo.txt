SEARCH AND SEIZURE MEMO (PANCHNAMA)

In connection with Case FIR No. CASE-0004 registered under Sections 302, 107, 109, and 120-B of the IPC, the undersigned Investigating Officer (IO), Sub-Inspector R.P. Singh, accompanied by a police team and two independent witnesses, namely Gopal Kara and one local resident known by the alias 'Kaka', proceeded to the residential premises of Manthan Chhabra located at 88, Desai Chowk, Darbhanga. The search commenced on 2026-06-25 at 09:30 hours in the presence of the suspect and the aforementioned witnesses. The purpose of the search was to recover material evidence regarding the conspiracy and alleged honour-based crime.

The following items were recovered, cataloged, and seized:

E-01: One blue mobile handset, make unspecified, bearing a number ending in 7187, found inside the bedside drawer in the master bedroom.
E-02: One set of silver-colored car keys labeled with a keychain bearing the partial plate …9788, recovered from the top of the dressing table.
E-03: A collection of handwritten documents and ledgers, including a paper slip referencing a transaction of 261,000 via NEFT to an account ending in …6226.
E-04: One Kingston 64GB USB flash drive, found hidden inside a hollowed-out book on the office desk.
E-05: Cash totaling 45,000 in various denominations, found in a locked wooden chest.
E-06: One diary with entries dating from June 2026, containing handwritten contact details for an individual identified as Aayush.

All recovered items were sealed in separate cloth parcels, each signed by the witnesses and the IO. The suspect, referred to locally as "Dada" by his neighbors, maintained a hostile demeanor throughout the proceedings. Items E-01, E-04, and E-06 have been earmarked for immediate dispatch to the Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL) for digital extraction and handwriting analysis. The search concluded at 13:15 hours on the same day. No damage was caused to the property during the search, and the suspect was provided a copy of this seizure memo.

Witness 1: Gopal Kara (S/o Late V. Kara)
