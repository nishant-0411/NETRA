REPORT:

I, Gopal Kara, currently residing at the aforementioned address, do hereby state that on the night of 24th June 2026, I arrived at the District Hospital, Lucknow, to visit an acquaintance. At approximately 22:07 hours, while passing through the peripheral parking area near the rear exit of the hospital, I observed a heated altercation involving two women, identified as Daksha Sachdev and Meghana Krishnan, and two men whose faces were partially obscured by shadows. 

One of the men, who appeared to be in his early seventies and was quite assertive in his tone, was heard by me explicitly mentioning the "sanctity of the family name" and demanding that the victims accompany him immediately. The other individual, a man of sturdier build, appeared to be acting as a lookout. I recognized the older man as a person often referred to locally by the alias "The Elder," who has previously been associated with community leadership circles.

The situation escalated rapidly. I observed a blue-colored hatchback, with a partial registration number ending in 9788, idling nearby with its engine running. As I moved closer, intending to intervene, I witnessed the two women being forcibly directed toward the vehicle. Fearing for my own safety, I retreated to a concealed position behind a pillar. Shortly thereafter, I heard muffled screams followed by the screeching of tires as the vehicle sped toward the North gate.

I immediately informed the hospital security, who conducted a sweep of the perimeter and subsequently discovered the bodies of Daksha S. and M. Krishnan in a desolate alleyway adjacent to the hospital wall. Both appeared to have sustained fatal injuries indicative of a violent struggle. 

Preliminary investigation suggests a coordinated conspiracy to commit murder under the guise of an "honour-related" dispute. Communication logs and tower dump analysis currently underway indicate that the mobile device of the primary suspect, a number ending in 7187, was active within the hospital vicinity at the time of the incident, corroborating the presence of individuals linked to the suspects known as Manthan and Aayush. 

I suspect that the motive is rooted in a long-standing familial vendetta, and the involvement of local influential figures cannot be ruled out. The vehicle, a TATA-make hatchback bearing the partial plate RJ-19...9788, is believed to be owned by the individual referred to as Manthan. I am providing this statement voluntarily and urge the authorities to take immediate action against the conspirators, as I fear for the safety of those involved in this matter.
