CD No. 1 | Date: 24/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Received information at 22:45 hours regarding two bodies found in a private vehicle parked outside the District Hospital, Lucknow. Upon arrival, identified victims as Daksha Sachdev and Meghana Krishnan. Injuries appear consistent with blunt force trauma and strangulation. Primary inspection of the vehicle, a blue hatchback, suggests a struggle occurred. Secured the perimeter and directed forensic team to lift prints. FIR 0004/26 registered under Sections 302, 107, 109, 120-B IPC. The vehicle, which bears a partial registration ending in 9788, has been seized. 

CD No. 2 | Date: 25/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Continued investigation at the crime scene. Recovered two mobile devices from the vehicle floorboard. One, a phone number ending in 7126, belongs to the deceased Sachdev. The second, a number ending in 3654, is linked to Meghana. Preliminary analysis of call logs shows multiple incoming calls on the evening of 24/06 from a number ending in 7187. Subscriber details for this suspect number are registered to one "M. Chhabra," residing in Darbhanga. We are currently verifying if this individual has any familial connection to the victims.

CD No. 3 | Date: 26/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Cross-referenced data from the cell tower dump near the hospital. The suspect number ending in 7187 was active within a 500-meter radius of the hospital between 21:30 and 22:30 on 24/06. Also detected a second suspicious number ending in 0141, registered to an Aayush Choudhury. A search of local property records indicates Choudhury is a close associate of Chhabra. Interrogated a witness, one Gopal Kara, who operates a tea stall nearby. Kara claims he saw a white motorcycle, registration plate partial …8890, speeding away from the hospital gate at 22:15. Checking RTO databases for this specific plate.

CD No. 4 | Date: 27/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Follow-up on the white motorcycle. It is registered to the witness, Gopal Kara, under number MH17VA8890. Brought Kara in for further questioning. He was visibly nervous and provided conflicting accounts of his whereabouts. He admits to knowing "The Sarpanch"—a local nickname for Manthan Chhabra—and claims he was hired to keep a watch on the victims’ movements. Kara alleges that he witnessed Chhabra and Choudhury arguing with the victims near the vehicle shortly before the incident occurred.

CD No. 5 | Date: 28/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Financial trail emerging. Scrutinized bank records and found a suspicious NEFT transfer of Rs 2,61,000 made on 10/06/2026 from an account belonging to Maanas Sarna to a PNB account ending in 6226. We are trying to establish the link between Sarna and Chhabra. Suspect Manthan Chhabra (alias "The Sarpanch") is currently evading police summons. His last known location, tracked via his device ending in 7187, was near the Kalyan-Dombivli border, suggesting he may be hiding with Aayush Choudhury. 

CD No. 6 | Date: 29/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Team dispatched to Kalyan-Dombivli to apprehend Choudhury. Found his residence empty. Recovered a handwritten note in the kitchen bin mentioning a meeting at "the old farm," which aligns with the suspect’s phone tower location at 02:00 this morning. As noted in CD No. 3, Choudhury’s movements have been consistent with Chhabra’s throughout the week of the murder. The forensic report regarding the struggle in the blue hatchback, RJ 19 OI 9788, has confirmed the presence of third-party DNA under the fingernails of both victims. We are moving to obtain warrants for the arrest of both Chhabra and Choudhury under the 120-B conspiracy charges.
