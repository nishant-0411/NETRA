CD No. 1  |  Date: 13/12/2025  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector S. K. Deshmukh
Received information regarding a suspicious heavy commercial vehicle at the Mumbai NH Checkpoint. Upon interception at 19:12 hours, the vehicle, a container truck, showed signs of tampering near the chassis. Initial search led to the discovery of synthetic narcotics concealed in custom-welded compartments. Driver present, but claims he is merely a contract hire. Seized contraband is being weighed and sealed. FIR registered under Sections 8(c), 21, 22, 29 of the NDPS Act and 120-B IPC. Driver’s mobile phone, a device ending in 2118, taken into possession for initial analysis.

CD No. 2  |  Date: 14/12/2025  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector S. K. Deshmukh
Interrogated the driver, Yatan Borah, who remains evasive. He claims he was hired by a man he only knows as "The Professor" to drive the vehicle from Pune to Gujarat. He denies knowledge of the narcotics. Analysis of the phone ending in 2118 shows multiple incoming calls from a number ending in 2488. CDR request submitted for both numbers to determine tower locations during the last 48 hours. A motorcycle, reportedly white, was observed tailing the truck on CCTV footage near the border, bearing a partial plate ending in …8890. 

CD No. 3  |  Date: 16/12/2025  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector S. K. Deshmukh
Telecom data received for the number ending in 2488. Subscriber identified as Chatresh Kohli, based in Solapur. Kohli’s location history aligns with the truck's transit route on the night of the 13th. Simultaneously, financial trail investigation initiated. Found an HDFC account belonging to a Gopal Kara that received a cash deposit of 55,000 INR on 11/11/2025. The transaction was facilitated from a source account linked to one Tanveer Gill. Gill appears to be the primary financier. The motorcycle seen in CD No. 2, registration MH-17-VA-8890, is registered in the name of the same Gopal Kara.

CD No. 4  |  Date: 18/12/2025  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector S. K. Deshmukh
Summoned Eiravati Zachariah for questioning. She is listed as an associate of the suspect network through a linked account ending in 19c2d. She appears nervous and claims she only provided financial management services for "small logistics firms." She denies knowing Tanveer or the man known as "The Professor." However, her device, ending in 3674, shows recurring communication with the number ending in 2488 (Kohli). There is a discrepancy in her statement regarding her location on the 13th; she claims to have been in Pune, but tower dumps suggest her phone was active in the vicinity of the checkpoint at 18:45, roughly 30 minutes before the seizure.

CD No. 5  |  Date: 20/12/2025  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector S. K. Deshmukh
Conducted a raid on the residence of Gopal Kara in Naihati. He was not present, but we recovered a ledger indicating payments to a "checkpoint official" whose identity remains masked. Cross-referenced this with the seizure at the Mumbai checkpoint. The note in the ledger mentions "The Professor" explicitly. Suspect Tanveer Gill’s location has been traced via his phone ending in 7139 to a remote area in Junagadh. Arrest teams dispatched. The motorcycle (MH17VA8890) has been impounded from a secondary location.

CD No. 6  |  Date: 22/12/2025  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector S. K. Deshmukh
Tanveer Gill apprehended in Junagadh. During preliminary interrogation, he admitted to transferring funds to Kara but maintains that these were for "legitimate interstate cargo transit." He refused to name "The Professor." When confronted with the CDR link between Zachariah and Kohli, he became agitated and requested legal counsel. We are now establishing the link between the seized narcotics in Mumbai and a recent similar seizure in a neighboring state, suspected to be part of the same supply chain controlled by Gill. Forensic audit of the ICICI account ending in 661f269 is pending.
