**Brief Facts of the Case:**

On 13/12/2025 at approximately 19:12 hours, during checking of a commercial heavy vehicle at the NH Checkpoint, Mumbai, the police team intercepted the vehicle and conducted an inspection.

During the inspection, a concealed secondary floor partition was found inside the trailer. Upon examination of the concealed compartment, approximately **14 kg of suspected narcotic substance** was recovered and seized.

The driver of the vehicle was detained and identified as **Yatan Borah**, aged approximately 17–18 years. During questioning, the driver gave contradictory information regarding the origin of the consignment and stated that the vehicle, bearing registration details ending in **8890**, had been handed over to him at a rest stop by an individual whom he referred to as **"The Uncle."**

A mobile phone recovered from the driver, bearing a number ending in **2118**, was taken into police custody.

The circumstances indicate suspected illegal transportation of narcotic substances through the intercepted commercial vehicle. The identity and involvement of other persons connected with the consignment are presently under verification.

The recovered suspected narcotic substance, vehicle and mobile phone have been taken into custody as per procedure.
