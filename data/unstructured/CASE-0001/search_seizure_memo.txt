SEARCH AND SEIZURE MEMO (PANCHNAMA)

In the matter of Case No. CASE-0001, registered under Sections 8(c), 21, 22, 29 of the NDPS Act and Section 120-B of the IPC, a search was conducted at the premises located at H.No. 418, Vyas Circle, Naihati, being the residence of the suspect identified as Gopal Kara, alias "The Runner". The search commenced on 2025-12-13 at 19:12 hours in the presence of independent witnesses, namely Jagat Mallick and Varenya Palla.

Prior to the commencement of the search, the undersigned Investigating Officer apprised the occupant and witnesses of their legal rights. The suspect, Gopal, was present during the duration of the search. Upon conducting a systematic search of the bedroom and attached storage loft, the following items were seized and sealed:

E-01: One mobile handset (Black body, make unknown) with a number ending in …2584. The device was found in a powered-on state and is currently logged into an account associated with the suspect.

E-02: A bundle of Indian Currency Notes (denominations of 500) totaling Rs. 85,000, recovered from a metal lockbox hidden beneath the bed frame.

E-03: Two vehicle keys bearing the logo of a two-wheeler, matching the registration plate sequence …8890, recovered from a drawer.

E-04: One handwritten ledger containing alphanumeric codes and entries corresponding to dates in November 2025, detailing transactions with an account ending in …3284.

E-05: One SanDisk 64GB USB flash drive, found concealed inside a hollowed-out book on the nightstand.

E-06: A set of photocopied documents including a driving license issued to Gopal Kara, bearing ID …fb40.

Items E-01 and E-05 have been packed and sealed with the official departmental seal to be dispatched to the Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL) for digital extraction and analysis of deleted logs and encrypted communications. The remaining items are to be deposited in the Malkhana for further investigation.

The search concluded at 21:45 hours. The contents of this Panchnama were read over to the witnesses in the vernacular language, who acknowledged the same as correct and true.
