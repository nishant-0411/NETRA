Report of the Incident:
I, Tejas Swamy, a resident of Cheema Path, hereby state that on 14/04/2025, at about 12:00 PM, I arrived at the District Hospital for a routine medical consultation. While walking towards the main entrance, I observed a heated altercation near a white motorcycle parked erratically near the staff gate. I recognized one of the individuals involved as a man who locals often refer to as "The Sarpanch’s Nephew," a person whose actual name I have come to know as Gopal. 

I witnessed Gopal, accompanied by another younger man who appeared to be driving a red sedan with a registration plate ending in 8690, confronting two men. The victims, later identified as Indrajit Bhatnagar and Tanveer Gill, were attempting to exit the premises. The situation escalated rapidly when the younger man in the red vehicle approached the victims with an iron object. I heard the men shouting about "family dignity" and "shame brought to the village," which led me to believe this was a premeditated act of honour-based violence. 

The victims were assaulted severely in the parking area. I retreated to the hospital security booth to raise an alarm. Upon returning with the security guards, the suspects had already fled the scene, with the motorcycle—bearing a registration plate beginning with MH17—speeding away towards the main arterial road. The victims were found unresponsive on the ground. Indrajit Bhatnagar appeared to be deceased on the spot, while Tanveer Gill showed signs of minimal life but succumbed to his injuries before emergency medical personnel could intervene. 

I suspect a broader conspiracy, as I have seen the individual known as Chatresh Kohli, a resident of Solapur, frequenting the area with these same suspects over the past week. It appears that the victim, Indrajit, had been under duress regarding his personal life and recent relocation. The suspects utilized encrypted communication to coordinate their movements at the hospital. 

I am unsure of the exact residential details of the culprits, but their vehicles are clearly identifiable. The red sedan, which I recall having a plate string like DL05…8690, was seen waiting near the hospital gate as early as 11:30 AM. I provide this statement in the presence of my witness, Ekiya Koshy, who was standing nearby during the incident. 

I request the police to investigate the links between the Sarpanch’s relative and the financial transactions that may have facilitated this attack. I fear for the safety of those involved in this matter.
