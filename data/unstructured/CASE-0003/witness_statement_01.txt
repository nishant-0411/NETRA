STATEMENT OF WITNESS UNDER SECTION 161 CrPC / BNS

I, Ekiya Koshy, daughter of late M. Koshy, aged 50 years, residing at 65/32, Tak Circle, Nashik, do hereby state the following before the Investigating Officer in relation to the incident involving the two men found at the District Hospital, Bengaluru. 

On the morning of April 14, 2025, I was present in the vicinity of the hospital gate to pick up some documents from a friend. I believe the time was approximately 12:20 PM when I noticed a disturbance near the parking area. I was standing near my parked vehicle when I saw two men in a heated argument. One of them, who I think was quite tall and wearing a dark jacket, was shouting at the other. I did not recognize either of them, but the second man looked very distressed and kept looking toward the hospital entrance.

While I was observing this, a red sedan drove up quickly and parked in a no-parking zone near the exit. I cannot be entirely certain of the full number, but I remember seeing the digits 8690 on the license plate. The car seemed to be a Maruti model. A man emerged from the driver’s side—I think his name was mentioned by the others as Gopal or something similar, though I might be misremembering—and he was acting very agitated. He walked toward the man in the jacket and they spoke in hushed tones for less than a minute. 

I then saw a white motorcycle, possibly a Royal Enfield, idling nearby. The rider, a younger-looking man, seemed to be signaling to the person from the red car. I am not entirely sure, but I think the rider of the motorcycle was wearing a helmet the whole time, so I could not get a clear view of his face. I felt uneasy because the atmosphere was quite tense, which is why I moved toward the main hospital building. When I looked back about five minutes later, the red car was pulling away, and the motorcycle had already disappeared toward the main road. 

The two men who were arguing had also moved away. I didn't think much of it at the time, assuming it was just a local dispute or a disagreement between patients' families. I am not entirely certain about the exact sequence of events, as I was quite distracted by my own work, but I am providing this statement to the best of my recollection. I have read the above statement and it is true to the best of my knowledge.
