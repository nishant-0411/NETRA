SEARCH AND SEIZURE MEMO (PANCHNAMA)

In connection with Case FIR No. 0003/2025 registered at Bengaluru Police Station under Sections 302, 107, 109, and 120-B of the IPC, the search of the premises belonging to the suspect Gopal Kara, situated at H.No. 418, Vyas Circle, Naihati, was conducted today, 16th April 2025, commencing at 09:30 hours. The search was conducted in the presence of two independent witnesses: Ekiya Koshy and Tejas Swamy.

The raiding team, led by the undersigned Investigating Officer, entered the premises after informing the occupant of the purpose of the search. Upon a thorough inspection of the living quarters, the following items were discovered, documented, and seized under a formal seizure memo:

Exhibit E-01: One mobile handset, black colour, make unidentified, found concealed inside a laundry basket in the bedroom. The device has a number ending in …584. 

Exhibit E-02: A bundle of documents including bank statements associated with an HDFC account belonging to Kara. The papers were found in a wooden cabinet.

Exhibit E-03: One set of ignition keys for a motorcycle with a registration plate partially read as MH…8890, found hanging near the entrance.

Exhibit E-04: A diary containing handwritten notes and ledger entries. Page 14 contains an entry referencing a transaction to an account number ending in …6918.

Exhibit E-05: One micro-SD storage card (64GB) recovered from a charging cable adapter found on the study table.

Exhibit E-06: Cash amounting to INR 45,000, kept in a sealed brown envelope, allegedly related to the “salary disbursement” mentioned in records.

All seized items were packed, labeled, and sealed with the official seal of the Investigating Officer in the presence of the witnesses. Items E-01 and E-05 are being forwarded to the Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL) for digital extraction and analysis of call logs and encrypted messaging fragments. The occupant, Gopal, was present during the entire duration of the search and has affixed his signature to the inventory list. The search concluded at 12:45 hours.
