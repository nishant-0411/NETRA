I, Pratyush Rajagopal, aged 43, do hereby state and declare on solemn affirmation as follows. On May 29, 2026, I was returning from my regular morning walk near the peripheral gate of the residential society in Jaipur. It was approximately 12:40 PM, or perhaps a few minutes earlier, when I noticed a white motorcycle, likely a Royal Enfield, parked near the secondary exit. I am not entirely certain about the exact registration, but I recall seeing the characters …8890 on the plate.

I observed a young man standing near the bike, appearing quite restless. He was pacing back and forth, constantly checking his phone. He looked to be in his early twenties, quite lean, and was wearing a dark windbreaker which seemed inappropriate for the heat. I believe someone referred to him as "Jagat" or perhaps it was "Jaggi," though I am not entirely sure as I was passing by at some distance. He kept looking towards the apartment complex block. 

A few minutes later, I saw a man—who I later learned was the victim, Wazir—walk out of the building lobby. He was on a call and seemed visibly agitated. As he reached the sidewalk, the young man on the motorcycle started the engine. The sound was distinct and loud. I walked toward the main gate and heard a heated exchange. I cannot be certain of the words used, but it sounded like a dispute over financial matters or perhaps a missing document. I remember hearing the victim mention "account numbers" or "transfers" quite loudly. 

I then turned the corner to head toward my residence, so I did not witness the actual incident. However, shortly after I reached my gate, I heard a sharp noise—like a metallic bang—followed by the sound of the motorcycle speeding away in the direction of the highway. I think I saw a second person in the vicinity, perhaps a woman, standing near the bushes, but I am not entirely sure if she was connected to the man on the bike. The whole scene felt very suspicious, but at the time, I assumed it was just a local delivery dispute. I am providing this information to the best of my recollection, though my sense of timing might be slightly off by ten or fifteen minutes due to the sudden panic that followed the noise. I have no personal enmity with any of the individuals involved.
