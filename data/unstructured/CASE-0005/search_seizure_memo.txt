SEARCH AND SEIZURE MEMO (PANCHNAMA)

In connection with FIR No. 05/2026, registered under Sections 302, 409, 420, and 34 of the IPC, the undersigned Investigating Officer, SI R. P. Singh, proceeded to the residence of the suspect, Jagat Mallick, located at a premium residential apartment complex in Jaipur, for the purpose of search and seizure. The search commenced at 14:00 hours on 30-05-2026 in the presence of independent witnesses, namely Pratyush Rajagopal (residing in Sikar) and Chaaya Krishna (residing in Guntakal).

Prior to entering the premises, the undersigned offered a personal search to the suspect, Jagat Mallick, which he declined. The search of the premises was conducted systematically in the presence of the suspect and the aforementioned witnesses. The following items were discovered, documented, and seized under the following exhibits:

Exhibit E-01: One black-colored smartphone of a popular make with a screen crack, bearing a SIM card and an IMEI number ending in …8821.
Exhibit E-02: A leather portfolio folder containing printouts of bank statements associated with an HDFC account belonging to B. Kibe, along with handwritten notes detailing NEFT transaction dates.
Exhibit E-03: One USB flash drive (128GB capacity) found in the study drawer, suspected to contain encrypted logs related to the shell company operations.
Exhibit E-04: A physical set of keys for a vehicle, attached to a keychain, corresponding to a two-wheeler with a partial plate registration ending in …8890.
Exhibit E-05: An envelope containing cash in various denominations, totaling approximately ₹4,50,000, which the suspect failed to explain the source of.
Exhibit E-06: A diary containing entries referring to an individual by the alias "The Handler," with timestamps corresponding to the day of the incident.

All items were sealed at the spot using official wax seals. Exhibits E-01 and E-03 have been marked for immediate transfer to the Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL) for digital extraction and data analysis. The search proceedings concluded at 17:30 hours. The suspect, Jagat, has been informed of the seizure, and copies of this Panchnama have been provided to him.
