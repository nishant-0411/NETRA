CD No. 1 | Date: 29/05/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R.K. Singh
Received information regarding a body found at a residential apartment in Jaipur. Proceeded to the scene. Victim identified as Wazir Swaminathan. Apartment locked from inside; forced entry made by society guards. Body found in study room with a single puncture wound to the carotid artery. No weapon found on site. Initial survey shows a laptop open to a corporate internal ledger. Called forensics team for fingerprint collection. Wazir’s mobile, ending in 5992, recovered from under the desk. Noted that the victim’s vehicle, a white sedan, was parked in its usual spot. FIR registered under Sec 302 IPC.

CD No. 2 | Date: 30/05/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R.K. Singh
Forensic report on the victim’s laptop suggests unauthorized access to company financial accounts dated three days prior. Analyzed call logs from phone ending in 5992. Found repeated communication with a contact saved as 'B. Kibe'. Initiated trace on this number, ending in 7019. CDR analysis shows the last outgoing call from Wazir was to this Kibe individual at 22:15 hours on the night of the incident. Visited the office of the victim. HR confirms a 'Bishakha Kibe' was a senior executive recently involved in internal audits.

CD No. 3 | Date: 01/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R.K. Singh
Bishakha Kibe, alias 'Bish', interrogated at the local station. She appears composed, claims her relationship with Swaminathan was strictly professional. Denies meeting him on the day of the murder. However, financial data retrieved today shows a transfer of 8 lakhs from her HDFC account ending in …6178 to an account in Solapur belonging to one Samaksh Raghavan. Bishakha claims this was a loan repayment. Discrepancy noted: the timeline of the transfer matches the dates Wazir flagged as 'suspicious' in his ledger.

CD No. 4 | Date: 03/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R.K. Singh
Acting on the digital trail, picked up a suspect, Jagat Mallick, from a hideout in Jhansi. Mallick is a known associate of the Solapur group. During questioning, he was evasive. Initially denied owning a vehicle, but CCTV footage near the crime scene in Jaipur shows a bike with a partial plate …8890, registered to a Gopal Kara. Mallick eventually admitted he was 'asked to watch' the premises by an associate of Bishakha. He denies the murder, claiming he only delivered a document. His phone, ending in …9b2e, is being sent for forensic recovery of deleted messages.

CD No. 5 | Date: 05/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R.K. Singh
Technical analysis of the device recovered from Mallick shows an exchange of location coordinates with Bishakha Kibe exactly two hours before the murder. As noted in CD No. 3, she had denied being in touch with any third party regarding the victim. Furthermore, the bike plate …8890 found in the CCTV footage is confirmed to be the same Royal Enfield registered to one Gopal Kara, who is currently employed as a driver for the Kibe household. The narrative of a simple "loan repayment" is falling apart. Adding Sec 409 and 420 IPC to the case file based on the embezzlement audit.

CD No. 6 | Date: 07/06/2026 | Investigating Officer: Inspector R.K. Singh
Confronted Bishakha with the financial trail to the Solapur account and the proximity of her driver’s bike to the crime scene. She shifted her stance, now claiming Wazir was blackmailing her. Her account of the night of 29/05/2026 contradicts the tower dump location data for her phone, which places her within a 1km radius of the society at the time of death, despite her claim that she was at a corporate dinner. Mallick has stopped talking, but we have enough evidence to seek judicial custody for both. Preparing the charge sheet draft. Case is moving toward a clear conspiracy between the executive and her hired support.
