STATEMENT UNDER SECTION 161 CRPC / BNS

I, Chaaya Krishna, daughter of late R. Krishna, aged 27 years, resident of 498, Thakkar Zila, Guntakal, do hereby state as follows:

On 29th May 2026, I was returning from my morning yoga class around 12:40 PM. I am certain about the time because I remember checking my watch right before entering the gates of the residential complex in Jaipur where the incident later came to light. 

As I was walking past the B-block parking area, I noticed a silver-coloured motorcycle parked somewhat erratically near the exit. I believe the last few digits of the registration plate were ...8890. It was a Royal Enfield, I think, but I am not an expert on bikes so I could be mistaken about the make. I saw two men standing near the vehicle. One of them, a younger man, looked quite restless. I did not get a clear look at the other person, but the younger one was tall and had a distinct scar or mark near his jawline. I remember hearing someone call him by the name "Jagat" or something very similar. 

The man who was later identified to me as Wazir Swaminathan was walking towards them, looking down at his mobile phone. He appeared agitated. I was about twenty-five feet away, moving towards my own car. I didn’t pay much attention initially, but I heard a sharp, raised voice—the man called Jagat sounded quite aggressive. He was asking something about "the transfers" and "why the account was frozen." I am not entirely certain about the exact words, but there was definitely a heated argument regarding some money. 

I then walked towards the elevator lobby. About ten minutes later, while waiting for the lift, I heard a loud thud, like something heavy falling, followed by a sudden silence. I did not hear any gunshot, though I suppose it could have been muffled by the building structure. I didn't see the actual attack; I assumed it was just someone moving furniture in one of the flats. I am not entirely sure, but I think I saw the same silver motorcycle speeding toward the main gate as I finally reached the lobby area. 

I am a bit shaken and my memory is fragmented due to the shock of learning about the death later that afternoon. I recall the younger man—the one called Jagat—wearing a dark blue jacket, although I might be confusing that with the colour of his helmet. Everything happened so fast.

I have read the above statement and it is recorded as per my version.
