Statement of Witness under Section 161 CrPC

I, Balveer Raja, S/o Late Shri Mohan Raja, aged 53 years, resident of 23/74, Kumer Chowk, Kirari Suleman Nagar, do hereby state on solemn affirmation that I am a local contractor and frequently visit the administrative blocks near the political office complex for my paperwork. On the date of October 12, 2024, I reached the vicinity of the office at approximately 14:20 hours. I remember looking at my wristwatch because I was worried about meeting a client before the office closed for the afternoon.

While I was walking near the main gate, I noticed a commotion occurring near the parking shed. I saw a young woman—I believe her name is Chaaya or someone similar—standing near her vehicle, appearing visibly distressed. There were two men arguing with her. I did not recognize them personally, but one of them, whom I heard the other man address as "Gopal," seemed to be the one doing most of the talking. He was quite aggressive, pointing his finger at the lady. I think I heard him mentioning something about "pending clearance fees" and "protection," but I cannot be entirely certain about the exact words due to the heavy traffic noise on the road.

Regarding the men, the one called Gopal appeared to be in his late twenties, quite thin, and was wearing a dark jacket. They had arrived on a white motorcycle. I recall glancing at the registration plate as they maneuvered to block the woman's path; I am fairly certain the plate ended in …8890, though I could be wrong about the letters preceding the digits. The other man, who looked slightly younger and stockier, was standing by a red sedan parked just a few meters away. I am not entirely sure if the red car belonged to them, but the younger man kept checking his phone—a device that seemed to have a cracked screen—and looking toward the office entrance as if he were waiting for a signal.

The entire incident lasted for about ten minutes. I felt nervous because of the way the man named Gopal was behaving, and I decided it was best not to intervene. I kept my head down and continued walking toward the gate. I didn't see the woman hand over any money, but the atmosphere was extremely tense. I left the area immediately after, and I cannot say for sure how they departed, though I heard the sound of a motorcycle engine roaring to life shortly after I turned the corner. I am providing this statement voluntarily and to the best of my recollection, though my memory of the exact sequence of events is a bit blurred due to the panic of the situation.

Signed,
(Balveer Raja)
Witness
