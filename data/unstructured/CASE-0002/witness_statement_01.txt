Statement of Witness recorded under Section 161 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS).

I, Nirja Khare, daughter of late Shri V. Khare, aged 19 years, residing at 475, Andra Zila, Phusro, state that on 12th October 2024, I was present near the political office complex in Lucknow. I had gone there around 14:15 or 14:20 hours to submit some documents related to my own registration work. While I was waiting near the main gate, I noticed a commotion occurring in the parking bay area. 

I observed a young woman, whom I later heard being addressed as Chaaya, standing near her vehicle. She seemed extremely distressed and was arguing with two men. I did not get a very clear look at their faces, but I recall one of them was quite tall and appeared to be giving orders. I think his name might have been Gopal, as I distinctly heard the other person shout that name while they were walking toward a motorcycle parked nearby. The motorcycle was white in color, and I believe the registration plate ended in digits something like 8890 or 8860. I am not entirely certain about the exact sequence, but I remember seeing those numbers.

The second man was slightly shorter and was pacing around a red sedan. I am not sure about the exact model, but the color was definitely a bright, almost deep red. I saw him holding a phone, constantly looking at it, and he seemed very agitated. I am not an expert on these matters, but the way they were speaking—it felt like a threat. At one point, the woman, Chaaya, was crying and saying something about money and bank transfers, though I could not catch the exact amounts. It sounded like an extortion demand, or perhaps they were forcing her to sign something under duress. 

I was standing about thirty feet away, trying to mind my own business, but the volume of their voices drew my attention. I think the incident must have lasted for about ten minutes before they all split up. The man I heard called Gopal rode off on the white motorcycle, and the other person drove away in the red car. I am not entirely sure if anyone else was watching, as the area was relatively quiet at that hour. I am a bit nervous about coming forward, but I have stated exactly what I witnessed to the best of my recollection. I have read the statement and it is true and correct.

(Signed)

Nirja Khare

Recorded by me,

Investigating Officer
Lucknow Police Station
