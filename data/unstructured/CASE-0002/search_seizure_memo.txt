SEARCH AND SEIZURE MEMO (PANCHNAMA)

In the matter of Case No. CASE-0002, pertaining to offences under Sections 384, 385, 120-B IPC and Sections 3, 4 of the PMLA, a search operation was conducted on 12-10-2024 at the premises identified as the "Political Office" located in Lucknow. The search commenced at 14:07 hours in the presence of witnesses Maanas Sarna and Balveer Raja. The primary suspect, Gopal Kara (also known as 'The Accountant' among associates), was present during the commencement of the search.

The following items were discovered, inventoried, and seized under the standard procedure of law:

E-01: One mobile handset, black colour, screen cracked, bearing IMEI ending in …9921. Device contains a SIM card registered to a number ending in 2584, currently subscribed to by Gopal Kara.

E-02: One hard-bound ledger book with red cover, containing handwritten entries of payments received from various construction entities. Entries dated August to October 2024 bear references to "Project Site-B".

E-03: Cash amounting to INR 4,50,000/- (Rupees Four Lakh Fifty Thousand) in denominations of 500, found concealed inside a hollowed-out wooden desk drawer.

E-04: A set of keys for a motorcycle, registration plate MH 17 VA 8890, observed parked in the adjacent alleyway.

E-05: One blue-coloured pen drive (32GB capacity) labeled "Private Records", containing digital spreadsheets titled "Money Flow".

E-06: A collection of photocopied documents regarding real estate shell firms, including property transfer agreements associated with an account ending in …6918 held by Jagat Mallick.

Items E-01 and E-05 have been sealed in specialized anti-static evidence bags and are being forwarded to the Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL) for digital extraction and analysis of deleted files. Item E-02 is being submitted for handwriting analysis to confirm the authorship of the ledger entries. 

The search concluded at 16:45 hours. All seized items were photographed in-situ before removal. The suspect Gopal Kara refused to sign the recovery memo despite being read the contents thereof.
