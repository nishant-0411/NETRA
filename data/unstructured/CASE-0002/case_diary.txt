CD No. 1  |  Date: 12/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Received a written complaint from Chaaya Krishna regarding organized extortion. The complainant, a contractor, alleges that she was coerced into paying protection money at a political office in Lucknow around 14:00 hours today. She claims a group of men threatened to halt her ongoing construction project if she did not comply. Registered FIR under sections 384, 385, and 120-B of the IPC. The victim appeared visibly shaken and mentioned that one of the suspects, whom she referred to as ‘The Ghost’ in her initial statement, had a distinctive tattoo on his neck. I have dispatched a team to secure the CCTV footage from the perimeter of the political office. Preliminary investigation suggests the involvement of an organized gang operating under the guise of real-estate consultants.

CD No. 2  |  Date: 13/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
We have secured the footage from the political office. A red sedan with a registration plate partially visible as DL-05-JE-XXXX was seen exiting the gate at 14:15 hours. The vehicle matches the description of a car associated with a suspect named Jagat, or Mallick as he is known in local records. I have instructed the cyber cell to pull the tower dump for the area covering the office at the time of the incident. We are tracking a mobile number ending in 66793 that pinged the local cell tower twice within a ten-minute window during the alleged extortion. The victim, Chaaya, has been called to the station to identify suspects from a dossier.

CD No. 3  |  Date: 15/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Chaaya Krishna visited the station and identified the person known as ‘The Ghost’ as Gopal Kara. She is terrified and claims she has been receiving anonymous threats via SMS since yesterday. I have directed the technical team to trace these messages. Meanwhile, the financial audit of shell accounts linked to the primary suspects has begun. We found an HDFC account belonging to Sharma—or Gopal, as the bank records confirm—that shows a suspicious transfer of Rs 7,90,000 to an ICICI account held by Jagat Mallick on 03/09/2024. The reference note says "Property advance," but we have no record of any such land deal.

CD No. 4  |  Date: 17/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Conducted a raid at the Naihati residence of Gopal. The suspect was not present, but we recovered a white Royal Enfield with registration MH-17-VA-8890. Neighbors mentioned that a man matching the description of Mallick was seen frequenting this address last week on a bike with plate MH17VA 8890. We are now invoking sections 3 and 4 of the PMLA. The evidence suggests the funds collected from contractors like Chaaya are being layered through multiple real-estate transactions before hitting accounts linked to Mallick.

CD No. 5  |  Date: 19/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
Examined the interrogation report of the witness Balveer Raja. He is an associate who has turned whistleblower. He confirms that the gang operates a fleet of vehicles, including the red Maruti sedan (DL05JE8690) noted in CD No. 2. Balveer claims the money laundering is orchestrated by ‘The Ghost’—Gopal—to distance himself from the physical threats made by Mallick. Balveer appears nervous and keeps looking over his shoulder; I have ordered protection for him as he is the key to linking the financial trail to the extortion threats.

CD No. 6  |  Date: 21/10/2024  |  Investigating Officer: Inspector R. K. Singh
The cyber cell submitted the CDR analysis for the number ending 66793. The user location data places the phone at the same location as Chaaya’s device on the day of the incident. We have also identified a third party, Maanas Sarna, whose account (No. ending 4465) received a small percentage of the funds transferred by Mallick. I am summoning Sarna for questioning tomorrow. The case is strengthening; we have the financial nexus, the vehicle movements, and the corroborating testimony from the whistleblower. The net is closing on Gopal and his associate.
