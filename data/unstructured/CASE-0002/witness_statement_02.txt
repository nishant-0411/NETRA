I, Maanas Sarna, aged approximately 57 years, son of late Sh. K. Sarna, residing at 46/488, Sarma Chowk, Khora, do hereby state and declare under oath as follows:

On October 12, 2024, I was present in the vicinity of the Political Office building in Lucknow for some personal documentation work. I believe the time was somewhere around 13:50 or 14:00 hours when I noticed a disturbance occurring near the main entrance gate. It was not a particularly busy hour, so the raised voices caught my attention almost immediately. 

I think I saw a woman, whom I later identified from the police photos as the complainant, Chaaya Krishna, arguing with two young men. She seemed very distressed and was clutching a folder of papers to her chest. One of the men, I believe someone called "Gopal" or perhaps "Goppie" by his companion, was being particularly aggressive. He was wearing a dark jacket and gesturing wildly with his hands. I am not entirely certain, but it looked like he was trying to snatch the files from her grip.

I observed a motorcycle parked haphazardly near the curb, barely a few feet from the argument. It was a white Royal Enfield, or something of a similar heavy build. I recall the registration plate ended in digits like …8890. The other man, who was standing slightly behind the first one, was keeping a constant watch on the street, occasionally pacing near a red sedan parked nearby. I didn't get a good look at the sedan's plates, but I am fairly sure the color was a very bright, distinct red. 

I stayed back because I did not want to get involved in what appeared to be a personal dispute, though the tone of their conversation was menacing. Gopal, or the man they called by that name, kept mentioning something about a "clearance" or "payment" that hadn't been processed. It sounded like they were demanding money from the lady. At one point, I thought I heard them threaten that if she didn't cooperate, her construction site work would be stalled indefinitely.

I cannot be entirely sure of the exact sequence of events after that, as I started moving away towards the bus stand, fearing they might notice me watching them. I feel like the confrontation lasted for about ten minutes before they pushed the woman toward her vehicle and then sped off. I am giving this statement of my own free will and volition to assist the investigation.
